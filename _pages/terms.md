This is a static academic website hosted through GitHub Pages.

## Information collected by this site

The site does not provide user accounts, a contact form, comments, advertising or first-party analytics. I therefore do not intentionally collect names, email addresses or other information directly through the website.

GitHub Pages and the infrastructure used to deliver the site may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs as part of normal hosting and security operations. Their handling of this information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## External and embedded content

Some pages include or link to services operated by third parties, including YouTube, Google Scholar, ORCID, INSPIRE--HEP, interactive maps and conference websites. When you open an external link or load embedded content, the relevant provider may receive technical information and may use cookies according to its own policies.
